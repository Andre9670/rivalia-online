Formulas · Max hit & block = ⌊(5 × skill + 50) × value × stance × 99 / 10000⌋ — attack stance 1.2, defense stance 0.6. A roll averages two 0–99% values, so the mean hit is about half the max. Armor reduces each physical hit by ⌊arm/2⌋ + rand(0…⌊arm/2⌋−1) = 0–0 for 0 armor — odd armor is wasted, so keep it even. Shield Defense values are best-effort 7.4 references.
