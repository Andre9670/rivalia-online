# Rivalia Discord - Server Save Update (pasted by Andrea, 20-21 Oct 2026)

Source: official Discord announcement, NOT yet on the wiki (charms.php still lists only 13 old charms as of 20 Oct 2026). One Charm Rune per creature.

## Encyclopedia Overhaul
New tabs: Bosses (replaces Daily Bosses, now with weekend bosses), Quests, Upgrading, Artifacts, Crafting, Gathering, Exaltation Forge, Elite Monsters, illustrated Game Guide. Spellbook live preview. Translated DE/ES/PL/PT/SV.
(Note: an "Exaltation Forge" tab now exists in the client - the Discord guide described in SKILL.md section B is therefore probably real.)

## New Artifacts
- Warriors Blessing - 5% chance to revive with full HP/mana after 5s. Hidden in the Nivarya vault.
- Cobra Spirit - from Ishebad (Ankrahmun) for a Helmet of the Ancients. +1 magic level and extra regeneration.

## 18 New Charms (cost in brackets; "hit" = chance per attack)
Utility:
- Fortune (800) - 2% on kill: creates an item from its loot, with attributes
- Tireless (300) - no stamina used for this monster
- Butcher (150) - +5% skinning and product drop chance
- Scholar (300) - +10% exp from this monster
- Recycler (200) - 10% chance runes/ammo aren't consumed
- Devotion (200) - 5% chance a kill counts twice (Bestiary & Task Board)
Combat:
- Sunder (220) - 4% hit: takes +12% damage for 6s
- Weaken (200) - 4% when it hits you: deals -25% damage for 6s
- Bleed (200) - 5% hit: 2% max HP/s for 6s, stacks x5
- Corrode (180) - 4% hit: no armor for 8s
- Chain Lightning (220) - 5% hit: 4% max HP energy damage, jumps to 2 nearby creatures of its kind
- Execute (250) - 5% to finish it off below 10% HP
- Soul Harvest (180) - 15% on kill: restore 3% max mana
Control:
- Petrify (280) - 3% hit: stunned 2.5s
- Frostbite (220) - 4% hit: rooted 3s; your next hit shatters it for 6% max HP
- Silence (250) - 4% hit: no spells for 5s
- Terror (220) - 3% hit: flees and stops attacking for 3s
On Kill:
- Haunt (300) - 5%: its spirit fights for you 10s at half strength
Execute, Control and Haunt don't work on bosses.

## Training Weapons (Store)
Sword, spear, wand, shield; 4 tiers; 10,000 charges; dummy-only, 80% power; faster attacks per tier, top tier 2% double hit; training shield + weapon +3%. New Training Stats channel; Skills window shows two decimals. x1/x3/x5 packs or with a Dummy Scroll.

## Royal Gold Pouch
50 coins for 7 days (stacks to 30, runs offline). Right-click -> "Deposit to bank" (not in combat/PZ). Becomes a normal pouch on expiry.

## Improvements
Guild Tasks side tracker; reworked Task Tracker; Item Stash: untick piles to keep on "Sell All"; Player Shops / Market Board full tooltips; Daily Reward shop sells Icicle Runes; Kongra/Merlkin/Sibang drop coconuts; Zarith buys Platinum Amulets for 2,000 gold; Safe Zone: +10% health/mana regen, no pushing on non-PvP tiles, 12h idle time for Premium.

## Balance
Player-cast Paralyze ignores the minimum speed floor.

## Bug fixes
Ground/house items keep bonus tiers and rarity after a save; login with trailing space; item attributes: per-slot and elemental resistances, dodge on any hit, parry only vs melee/distance; client crash on damaged minimap file.
