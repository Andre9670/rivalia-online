# The Djinn War - Marid Faction

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: rewards/dangers/NPCs MAY differ — verify in-game or on wiki.rivaliaonline.com.

## Quest Info

- **Implemented:** 7.4
- **Premium:** yes
- **Location:** Ashta'daramai (Blue Djinn Fortress)
- **Level (required):** 40
- **Level (recommended):** 50+
- **Also known as:** Blue Djinn Quest
- **Reward:** 3 Small Sapphires, Gemmed Lamp, the ability to trade with Nah'Bob and Haroun, possibility to hunt Efreet and Green Djinns in Ankrahmun and Yalahar and possibility to start a green djinn hunting task.
- **Dangers:** Green Djinns, Efreets, Dragon, Orc Warlords, Orc Leaders, Gargoyles, Orc Berserkers, Stone Golems, Stalkers, Orc Warriors, and a Wyvern in the path of the secret entrance of green djinn fortress. Also minor creatures like Cobras, Lions and Hyaenas in the path.
- **Legend:** Ally with the Marids, an ancient race of Djinns to obliterate their counterparts, the fearsome Efreets, and gain eternal trust from those powerful creatures.
