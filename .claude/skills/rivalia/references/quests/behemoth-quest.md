# Behemoth Quest

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: rewards/dangers/NPCs MAY differ — verify in-game or on wiki.rivaliaonline.com.

## Quest Info

- **Implemented:** 7.2
- **Premium:** yes
- **Location:** Edron (Cyclopolis)
- **Level (required):** 80
- **Level (recommended):** 80
- **Also known as:** Guardian Halberd Quest, Cyclopolis Quest
- **Reward:** Demon Shield, Golden Armor, Guardian Halberd, Platinum Amulet, Life Ring, Crystal Ring, 3 Small Diamonds, 4 Small Sapphires
- **Dangers:**
  - Cyclops Drones, Cyclops Smiths, Dwarf Guards, Dwarf Soldiers, Fire Elementals, Dragons and a Dragon Lord on the way to the quest.
  - 4 Behemoths, 2 Cyclops Smiths and 2 Dragons in the quest room.

## History

The level requirement for this quest was changed from 60 to 80 in late 2004 or in the beginning of 2005.
