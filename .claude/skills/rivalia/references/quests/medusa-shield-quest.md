## Quest Info

- **Implemented:** 7.3
- **Premium:** yes
- **Location:** Drefia west of Darashia
- **Level (required):** 60
- **Level (recommended):** 70
- **Also known as:** Star Room Quest, Necromancer Quest, Blue Robe Quest, Goshnar Quest
- **Reward:** Medusa Shield, Skull Staff, Blue Robe
- **Dangers:** Necromancers, Blood Priests, Blood Hands, Demon Skeletons, Mummies, Vampires, Priestesses, Zombies, Skeletons, Rats, possibly Necropharus (rare spawn), and other Undead creatures.
- **Legend:** The chief Necromancers of the Brotherhood of Bones hold a valuable treasure here.
