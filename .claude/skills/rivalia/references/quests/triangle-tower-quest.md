# Triangle Tower Quest

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: rewards/dangers/NPCs MAY differ — verify in-game or on wiki.rivaliaonline.com.

## Overview

- **Premium:** no
- **Reward:** Garlic Necklace, Dwarven Ring and 2 Small Sapphires
- **Location:** Triangle Tower
- **Level:** 0
- **Recommended level:** 40
- **Dangers:** Skeletons, Ghouls, Demon Skeletons, Stalkers, a Witch, a Monk and a Dwarf Geomancer. If you walk downstairs, below ground level you may meet a Bonelord.
- **Legend:** Three users of magic plot in a tower filled with undead.
