## Quest Info

- **Implemented:** 6.4
- **Premium:** yes
- **Location:** Edron Hero Cave
- **Level (required):** 100
- **Level (recommended):** 120+
- **Also known as:** Demon Quest
- **Reward:** Demon Outfits' first addon as well as Demon Helmet, Demon Shield and Steel Boots.
- **Dangers:** 4 Demons, 8 Banshees in the quest room; 3 Dragons, 5 Demons, multiple Priestess, Demon Skeletons, Wild Warriors, Bonelords, Minotaur Mages, Hero, Orc Shaman, Monks and Fire Elementals on the way there.
