## Quest Info

- **Premium:** no
- **Location:** Elvenbane Castle, near Ab'Dendriel
- **Level (required):** 0
- **Level (recommended):** 35+
- **Also known as:** Elf Castle Quest
- **Reward:** Morning Star, Dwarven Shield, Strong Mana Potion, Blank Rune, Spellbook, 2 Small Diamonds and 100 gp.
- **Dangers:** Orcs, Orc Warriors, Orc Berserker, Orc Spearman, Minotaurs, Minotaur Guards, Dragon, Orc Shaman, Minotaur Mage, Demon Skeleton and Elf Arcanist.
- **Legend:** An Elf Arcanist hoards treasure in his highly fortified castle.
