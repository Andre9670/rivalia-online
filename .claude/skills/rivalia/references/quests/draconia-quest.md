# Draconia Quest

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: rewards, dangers (creature stats/loot), and NPC behavior MAY differ — verify in-game or on wiki.rivaliaonline.com before relying on rewards.


- **Reward:** Ice Rapier, Serpent Sword, Stone Skin Amulet and Energy Ring.
- **Location:** Draconia, through Hellgate, near Ab'Dendriel
- **Required Level:** 25
- **Recommended Level:** 25+
- **Dangers:** Skeletons, Ghouls, Demon Skeletons, Mummies and a possible lure of one or more Elder Bonelords.
- **Premium:** no
- **Implemented version:** 6.2

## Overview

You will need to solve riddles to get the keys and go up in the pyramid.
