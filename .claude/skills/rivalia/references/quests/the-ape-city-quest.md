# The Ape City Quest

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.6 VANILLA** (implemented 7.6, i.e. *after* the 7.4 cut-off of the other quests in this folder). What IS confirmed on Rivalia's own data: Banuta, its underground floors and the "Deeper Banuta" floors exist (see *On Rivalia* below).


- **Also known as:** Banuta Quest, Ape Quest, Monkey Quest, King Bong Quest, Big Bong Quest, Forbidden Lands Quest
- **Reward:** Healing by Hairycles, ability to buy Bananas for 2 gp, the three Monkey Statues (No Hearing / No Seeing / No Speaking), Shaman Outfits, permission to go to the Forbidden Lands and access to Deeper Banuta.
- **Location:** Various; begins in Tiquanda (Hairycles' temple, south-east Banuta).
- **Required Level:** 0
- **Recommended Level:** 130+
- **Dangers:** Dworcs, Apes, Lizards, Spit Nettles, Skeletons, Ghouls, Fire Devil, Scarabs, Carniphilas, Gargoyles, Stone Golems, Bonelords, Green Djinns, Crypt Shambler, Demon Skeletons, Elder Bonelords, Efreets, Bonebeasts, Giant Spiders, Serpent Spawns, Hydras, Behemoths.
- **Premium:** yes

## Overview

Hairycles, the Merlkin shaman, wants proof that humans can be true friends of the apes. Nine missions; **accepting mission 9 gives you the Snake Destroyer and opens the way into Deeper Banuta.**

## Equipment

Shovel, Rope, Cough Syrup (buy in advance), Hydra Egg (buy in advance), Key 3940, Crowbar, a melee weapon (to break amphoras — the Snake Destroyer cannot), Dwarven Ring (one per team member for mission 9), and a lot of travel money.

## Missions

1. **Whisper Moss** — Dworc Caves: in Trapwood (south Tiquanda) fall down the south-easternmost pitfall, hit the damaged wooden bars with a melee weapon, open the box behind the sealed door in the east of the room.
2. **The Cure** — Cough Syrup (50 gp) from Padreia in Carlin; Brewster → Ustan → Padreia chain. Can be bought in advance or given by another player.
3. **Lizard Parchment** — Chor: enter by the south-western log, go south over the bridge, then east; sealed door on a building's east side; box holds the Old Parchment. Many lizards — a Great Fireball mage helps; kill Snakecharmers first.
4. **Parchment Decyphering** — **Shadow Tomb**: dig the entrance, go to the second main floor (the one with the blue flame), south from the ramp then east to the end: an Obelisk and 4 Red Shrine Stones. Step next to the Obelisk to decipher the scroll.
5. **Hydra Egg** — bring any Hydra Egg (common Hydra loot, tradable; also the Hydra's Nest in the Hydra Egg Quest).
6. **Witches' Cap Spot** — Fibula Dungeon, Bonelord section; needs Key 3940 (800 gp from Simon the Beggar or 2,000 gp from Dermot). Use the Draw Well in Fibula to enter. After this you are a "friend of the apes" (Bananas 2 gp, free healing from Hairycles). The Cap Spot lasts 24 h.
7. **Destroying Casks** — Crowbar; from Hairycles go down twice, north through the apes' room, west down the stairs, then east/north/west/north to the room with 7 casks. Destroy 3 casks per person (they respawn in a few minutes).
8. **Evidence of Holy Ape** — the Blind Prophet (east of Banuta) transports you to the Forbidden Lands; go west along the southern palisade, north to a hole (Hydra cave), then north-east to two Giant Footprints; use the north-western tile of the left one for the Giant Ape's Hair (replaceable from Black Bert). Hardest part before the last mission.
9. **The Deepest Catacombs** — you receive the Snake Destroyer and entry to Deeper Banuta. Enter the Magic Forcefield (teleporter) on Banuta floor -2. On the first Deeper Banuta floor break the 4 Canopic Jars (they respawn — be quick) to open the central teleporter; on the quest floor pull all 7 levers to the right (the one by the sealed wall last), then use the Snake Destroyer on The Statue of the Snake God in the north room. Report to Hairycles and ask for "shamanic powers" for the Shaman Outfit.

**Shortcut:** bring an Egg of the Many to Deeper Banuta and use it on the snake head directly east of the teleporter — it unlocks a permanent secret tunnel from Tiquanda (vanilla coordinates x 32853, y 32669, surface) so you no longer need the jar teleporter.
