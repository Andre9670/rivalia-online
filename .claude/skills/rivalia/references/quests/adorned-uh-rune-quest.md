> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: rewards/dangers/NPCs MAY differ — verify in-game or on wiki.rivaliaonline.com.

## Quest Info

- **Implemented:** 6.4
- **Premium:** yes
- **Location:** Edron Hero Cave.
- **Level (required):** 0
- **Level (recommended):** 40
- **Also known as:** Triple UH Rune Quest
- **Reward:** Silver Rune Emblem (Ultimate Healing).
- **Dangers:** Hard creatures, including Heroes, Priestesses, Wild Warriors, Demon Skeletons, Bonelords, Monks, Hunters and Dragons.

## History

With the Updates/8.6 the overcharged runes were removed from tibia and replaced with golden and silver Rune Emblems. This resulted in the reward of this quest being changed from an Ultimate Healing Rune with 3 charges, to a Silver Rune Emblem (Ultimate Healing).
