# The Queen of the Banshees Quest

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: rewards, dangers (creature stats/loot), and NPC behavior MAY differ — verify in-game or on wiki.rivaliaonline.com before relying on rewards.


- **Also known as:** Banshee Quest
- **Reward:** Boots of Haste, Giant Sword, Tower Shield, Stealth Ring, Stone Skin Amulet and 100 Platinum Coins (10K)
- **Location:** Isle of the Kings underground and Ghostland
- **Required Level:** 60
- **Recommended Level:** 80+
- **Dangers:** Demon Skeletons, Ghosts, Warlocks, 3 Giant Spiders and of course Banshees (through the seals). The final room involves 2 Dragon Lords, 2 Giant Spiders and 6 Banshees.
- **Premium:** no
- **Implemented version:** 7.2

## Overview

You have to complete 7 seals to get to the final room with the rewards. **Do not** enter the third floor on Isle of Kings or you will have to pay a certain amount of money. Teleporting out means you have to get on the isle using the pearl seal.
