# The Paradox Tower Quest

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: rewards, dangers (creature stats/loot), and NPC behavior MAY differ — verify in-game or on wiki.rivaliaonline.com before relying on rewards.


- **Also known as:** Riddler Quest, Mathemagics Quest
- **Reward:** Up to two of the following: 10k gp, Wand of Cosmic Energy, 32 Talons and Phoenix Egg. You will also get the achievement Mathemagician.
- **Location:** Paradox Tower near Kazordoon.
- **Required Level:** 30
- **Recommended Level:** 50+
- **Dangers:** Wyverns, (Mintwallin): Minotaurs, Minotaur Archers, Minotaur Guards, Minotaur Mages, (Hellgate): Skeletons, Ghouls, Bonelords, maybe Elder Bonelord, 
(Plains of Havoc): Skeletons, Ghouls, Demon Skeletons, Orc Berserker, Orc Spearman, maybe Cyclops and Giant Spider
- **Premium:** yes
- **Implemented version:** 6.61-6.97

## Overview

Surpass the wrath of a madman and subject yourself to his twisted taunting.

## History

It was originally implemented with the Darashia Update, before the quest system existed. Back then, the possible rewards were a Red Spell Wand, a Time Ring, a Magician Hat and a Violet Gem. With Update 7.24, almost 2 years after the quest had been implemented, its rewards were changed to 10k gp, a Wooden Wand, 32 Talons and a Phoenix Egg. Lastly, one and a half years later, the wooden wand was replaced by a Wand of Cosmic Energy on Christmas Update 2005.
