# Exchange Quests

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: rewards/dangers/NPCs MAY differ — verify in-game or on wiki.rivaliaonline.com.

## Overview

- **Type:** exchange
- **Reward:** Exchange dropped items for Quest Items
- **Location:** Various
- **Level:** 0-35
- **Recommended level:** 8-20
- **Dangers:** Depends on specific quest. Some creatures: Rotworms; Scorpions, Hyaenas, Cobras, Nomads; Tigers, Centipedes.
- **Legend:** You can acquire Quest Items by giving NPCs items dropped by monsters.
