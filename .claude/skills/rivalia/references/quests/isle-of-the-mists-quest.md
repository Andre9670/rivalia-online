- **Premium:** no
- **Also known as:** Druid Quest
- **Reward:** 2 Small Emeralds, Book
- **Location:** Isle of the Mists, Near Kazordoon and Venore
- **Level:** 0
- **Recommended level:** 40
- **Dangers:** Fernfang spawns here.
- **Legend:** Some tibian vocations have special places, where only that vocation can enter. Druids are not an exception. If you are a druid, try to find your own place and you will receive your prize.
