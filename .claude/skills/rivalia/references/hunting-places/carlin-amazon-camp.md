:*This page is about the Amazon Camp near Carlin. For the Amazon Camp near Venore, see Venore Amazon Camp.*

The **Carlin Amazon Camp** is smaller than the one near Venore. It is a common strategy to hop between this camp and the Amazon Tower north of Carlin  if the monsters are killed faster then they spawn back. It is a good place for low-level players to make money by looting Protective Charms and selling them to other players for Imbuing.

**Monsters in Carlin Amazon Camp:**

- Amazon
- Bear
- Spider
- Valkyrie
