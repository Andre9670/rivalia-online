## Overview

- **Location:** North of Rookgaard
- **Nearest City:** Rookgaard
- **Recommended Level:** Knights 6, Paladins 6, Mages 6
- **Vocation:** No Vocations
- **Profit / Exp:** Loot/Profit: Very Bad (★); Exp: Very Bad (★)
- **Notable Loot:** Mace, Plate Shield, Chain Armor, Minotaur Leather, Bronze Amulet
- **Implemented:** 5.2

**Minotaur Hell** is a cave that lies north of Rookgaard. It can be accessed through the main Rookgaard cave, (here — 125.94/125.137/7). You will meet Snakes, Wolves, Trolls, Orcs and Minotaurs on the way to Minotaur Hell. It is a dangerous place to hunt alone, therefor it is recommended to bring a team. But if hunting alone, a good idea to take a box and let only one or two attack at a time. There is a Minotaur Mage visible in a room to the west, although it is unreachable. There is a book regarding his presence there.

Since the Christmas Update 2006 a boss creature, Apprentice Sheng has a chance of spawning.

The Minotaur Hell Quest is located here.
