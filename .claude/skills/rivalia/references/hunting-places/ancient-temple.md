## Overview

- **Location:** North of Thais, (here — 126.100/125.170/7).
- **Nearest City:** Thais
- **Recommended Level:** Knights 8, Paladins 13, Mages 8
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Recommended Skills:** Knights 30, Paladins 30
- **Profit / Exp:** Loot/Profit: Varies; Exp: Varies
- **Notable Loot:** Dragon Shield, Wand of Inferno, Dragonbone Staff, Terra Rod, Wand of Cosmic Energy
- **Implemented:** 4.0

**Sub-areas / deeper levels:**
- For Slimes — rec. level Kn 15, Pal 12, Mag 13

The **Ancient Temple** is said to have been built in order to honour Banor after a great victory over the Orcs. Ironically, it's them who now occupy large sections around this temple.

Located just north west of Thais north gate, this is a good hunting place for players of various levels. Various minor quests can be found while exploring the tunnels under the temple. This was a good place to find unexpected dragons, giant spiders and trap-player killers before a couple of updates fixed the problem. Although, it is still possible to lure a Fire Devil (on the same floor as rotworms). For the place with Slimes, which is very good to train your skills.

*see also:* Ancient Temple/Maps, Mintwallin, Thais Dragon Lair, Mad Mage Room

Some Quests are located here, as: Scale Armor Quest, Into the Bone Pit Quest, Life Ring Quest, Devil Helmet Quest, Ghoul Room Quest, Mad Mage Room Quest, Mintwallin Cyclops Quest, Old Mintwallin Quest, Throwing Star Quest, Naginata Quest, Six Rubies Quest.
