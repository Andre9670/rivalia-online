# The Arcanum

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

The Arcanum is a stage in the Demon Forge and is the route to The Soul Wells. This is the first stage leading to the demon boss Annihilon. Players arrive here after navigating The Battlefields and slaying the two demon bosses Latrivan and Golgordan. There is an underlying theme of magic in this area.

**See also:** Demon Forge, The Battlefields, The Soul Wells, Annihilon
