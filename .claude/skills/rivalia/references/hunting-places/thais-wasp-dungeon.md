# Thais Wasp Dungeon

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

- **City:** Thais
- **Location:** Southeast of Thais. The entrance is hidden by a tree.
- **Recommended level:** Knights 10, Paladins 10, Mages 12
- **Experience:** Average (3/5)
- **Profit/Loot:** Bad (1/5)
- **Notable loot:** Honeycomb

The cave extends for two levels and is full of wasps. You must have an antidote for poison and healing spells or potions, because you will certainly be surrounded by wasps and poisoned.

It is not a good hunting place, considering the high risk of being killed by the wasps, but is a good place if one is looking for honeycombs.

**Ground level creatures around cave:**

- Wasp
