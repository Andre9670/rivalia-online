**Ferngrims Gate** is an area that is located west of Rorc Plains which is inhabited by various creatures. There is a Wyvern hill (here) between the Gate and the Rorc area with very fast respawn rate. A different Wyvern hill is located here which has 3 Wyverns. The dragons at Ferngrims Gate are located at the Kazordoon Dragon Lair (here) and a single dragon is located here.
