## Overview

- **City:** Thais
- **Location:** East of Thais, South of Kazordoon, Northwest of the Desert Dungeon, (here (coords 127.50, 125.120)).
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Recommended level:** Knights 45 / Paladins 40 / Mages 40
- **Skills:** Knights 65 / Paladins 65
- **Experience:** Average ★★★
- **Profit / Loot:** Bad ★★
- **Best loot:** Stuffed Toad, Witch Hat, Terra Boots, Terra Rod, Bag of Apple Slices
- **Creatures:** Bonelord, Skeleton, Ghoul, Demon Skeleton, Stalker, Witch, Monk (Creature), Dwarf Geomancer

**Triangle Tower** is a daunting place to approach. Not much is known about its history, but there must be a fantastic story because of the variety of dangerous creatures that can be found there. On the top floor the masters of the tower can be found, a bizarre alliance of a Dwarf Geomancer, a Witch, and a Monk. To open triangle tower you use a switch in the desert. This switch is across the river from the tower, hidden beneath some rubble, here (coords 127.61, 125.121). It's a long walk to reach the switch, so you should check if the tower is open first. In case the tower is closed while you are inside, use the teleporter on the basement level to get out, but beware; there is a Bonelord down there as well. 

The Triangle Tower Quest takes place here, as well as Mission 5 of the What a Foolish Quest.
