## Overview

- **City:** Thais
- **Location:** Thais, near south gate, here.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Recommended level:** Knights 20 / Paladins 20 / Mages 15
- **Skills:** Knights 50 / Paladins 50
- **Experience:** Bad ★★
- **Profit / Loot:** Very Bad ★
- **Best loot:** Iron Helmet, Brass Armor, Bat Wing
- **Creatures:** Bandit, Bat

The Bandit Cave in Thais is a little hole near southern gate. Here you will find 2 Bandits and 2 Bats.

To enter, you need use a Shovel on the Loose Stone Pile here. To leave the cave you can use the southern stairs where you came in or the northern stairs, to back to Thais.

Note: If you uses northern stairs, you can't enter again from there. The unique entrance is from Loose Stone Pile out Thais.
