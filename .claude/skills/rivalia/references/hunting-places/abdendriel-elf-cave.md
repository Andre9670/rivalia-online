There is a cave north-west of Ab'Dendriel where a number of elves live. It is somewhat difficult to find, and you will need a Machete or a Heavy Machete to reach it.

There are two ways to access this cave, both ways require a Machete or a Heavy Machete.

*From the North:
** Fall down this hole (x:127.134, y:123.108, z:7)
** Walk underground, you will face 2 Bats. You will come up (x:127.115, y:123.115, z:7)
*From the South:
** You can walk around the south side of the swamp area ((x:127.107, y:123.149, z:7)) and use your Machete/Heavy Machete to cut down the Jungle Grass.

Now walk to the cave (x:127.134, y:123.131, z:7). 

There are about 6 Snakes and 3 Elves on the surface.

Up the rope spot you will find 3 Elves. There is a total of 7 Elves, 4 Elf Scouts and 1 Elf Arcanist in the dungeon. Knights, be careful: If you chase an Elf Scout, there is a good chance you will attract more enemies, and they can easily kill you.
