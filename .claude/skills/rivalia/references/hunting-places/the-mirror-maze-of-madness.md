# The Mirror Maze of Madness

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

The Mirror Maze of Madness (official name, also simply called The Mirror Maze), is a maze stage of the Demon Forge and is the route to the The Sunken Cave. This is the second stage leading to the demon boss Zugurosh. Players arrive here after navigating The Crystal Caves.

**See also:** Demon Forge, The Crystal Caves, The Sunken Cave, Zugurosh
