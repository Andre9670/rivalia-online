## Overview

- **Implemented:** 7.3
- **City:** Ankrahmun
- **Location:** Below the Ankrahmun Library, (x:129.118, y:128.70, z:7).
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Recommended level:** Knights 10 / Paladins 10 / Mages 13
- **Recommended skills:** Knights 45 / Paladins 45
- **Recommended defence:** Knights 40
- **Loot:** Unknown
- **Experience:** Unknown
- **Notable loot:** Holy Scarab, Maxilla Maximus, Mini Mummy, Hammer of Wrath, Boots of Haste


The deeper you go, the harder the monsters are. In order to get past the teleporter you should stand in the Mystic Flame and put a Scarab Coin on the Empty Coal Basin . Be careful though, any creature next to the Mystic Flame on the other side will be attacking you as soon as you appear.
