# The Vats

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

## Overview

- **Creatures:** Son of Verminor, Plaguesmith, Blightwalker, Defiler

The Vats is a poison-themed stage in the Demon Forge and is the route to The Battlefields. This is the first stage leading to the two demon bosses Latrivan and Golgordan. Players arrive here after slaying Madareth in The Blood Halls.

See also: Demon Forge, The Blood Halls, The Battlefields, Latrivan, Golgordan
