# The Hive (Demon Forge)

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

:*Not to be confused with The Hive, insectoid habitat of Quirefang.*
**The Hive** is the last part of the Demon Forge. This is the first and only stage leading to the demon boss Hellgorak. Players arrive here after navigating The Soul Wells and will appear in The Shadow Nexus once Hellgorak has been slain.

**See also:** Demon Forge, The Soul Wells, The Shadow Nexus, Hellgorak
