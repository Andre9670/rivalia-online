The **Shadow Tomb** (officially **Mahrdis' Cauldron**) is a hidden tomb east of the Ankrahmun city. The tomb has a fire theme and a lot of the creatures within the tomb are fire-based, including the pharaoh Mahrdis found at the end of the tomb. There are enemies that turn invisible so area of effect runes or cancel invisibility is recommended.

The name of this Tomb is given because it lies in the shadows of the south-eastern tower of the city. This information can be found in this book.
