# The Soul Wells

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

## Overview

- **Creatures:** Betrayed Wraith, Spectre, Lost Soul, Phantasm, Demon

The Soul Wells is a stage in the Demon Forge and is the route to the demon boss Annihilon. This is the second and final stage leading to the demon boss. Players arrive here after navigating The Arcanum and will gain access to the Hive once Annihilon has been slain.

See also: Demon Forge, The Arcanum, The Hive (Demon Forge), Annihilon
