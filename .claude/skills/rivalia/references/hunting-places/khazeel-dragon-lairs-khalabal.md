The **Kha'zeel Dragon Lairs/Kha'labal** are a common hunting spot. They are very similar to the Kha'zeel Dragon Lairs/Tiquanda, but are accessed through the Kha'labal instead of Tiquanda. 

For mages level 28 it is advised to summon one or two Demon Skeletons to use as blockers since they are immune to fire and attack with exori frigo or other ice attacks. They can be healed with cheap intense healing runes, or massive healing.
## South Dragon Lair
This is the easiest of the three Ankrahmun Dragon Lairs and is situated (x:129.36, y:127.224, z:7).

1st floor has 1 dragon and two Scorpions.

2nd floor has 2 dragons.

3rd floor has 3 dragons.

**Creatures in South Dragon Lair**

- Scorpion
- Dragon

Tip for mages:They can go from lvl 28 using exori frigo and icicle rune .
## Middle Dragon lair
This is a harder lair than the south one and situated (x:129.71, y:127.178, z:7).

More Commonly known as the Ank 3x Spawn

1st floor has 3 dragons, which can be lured one by one.

North hole has 1 dragon lord and 2 dragons.

South hole has 2 dragons.

2nd floor has 4 dragons and 1 dragon lord.

3rd floor has 2 dragon lords.

**Creatures in Middle Dragon Lair**

- Dragon
- Dragon Lord
## North Dragon Lair
This is the hardest of the three Ankrahmun Dragon Lairs and situated (x:129.92, y:127.94, z:7).

More Commonly known as the Ank 9 Spawn

Ground level has 2 Dragon Lords and 2 Dragons which can be lured one by one

1st floor has two respawns. South respawn has 3 dragons, which can be lured one by one. North respawn has 4 dragons  which can be lured one by one.

2nd floor has 4 dragons and 4 dragon lords.

3rd floor has 2 dragon lords.

4th floor has 8 Fire Elementals.

**Creatures in North Dragon Lair**

- Fire Elemental
- Dragon
- Dragon Lord
