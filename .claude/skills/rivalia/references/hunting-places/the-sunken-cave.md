# The Sunken Cave

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

## Overview

- **Creatures:** Massive Water Elemental, Quara Pincher, Quara Predator, Quara Hydromancer

The Sunken Cave is the underwater stage of the Demon Forge and is the route to the demon boss Zugurosh. This is the third and final stage leading to the demon boss. Players arrive here after navigating The Mirror Maze of Madness and gain access to The Blood Halls once Zugurosh has been slain.

There are no demonic creatures here - just the usual underwater creatures although The Ruthless Seven may have future plans with the Quara.

See also: Demon Forge, The Mirror Maze of Madness, The Blood Halls, Zugurosh
