## Overview

- **City:** Kazordoon
- **Location:** East of the dwarven bridge, here.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Experience:** Average (3/5)
- **Loot:** Average (3/5)
- **Notable loot:** Wand of Cosmic Energy, Taurus Mace, Purple Robe, Silver Amulet, Minotaur Horn

### Recommended Levels & Skills

- **Knights:** Level 8+, skill 20, def 20
- **Paladins:** Level 8+, skill 20, def 20
- **Mages:** Level 9+

**Dusalk's Troll Clan Cave** (officially named on the map "Kazordoon Troll Den") is located between Kazordoon and Venore, east of the Dwarven Bridge, you need a shovel to open the hole that is hidden behind a tree. This cave includes many Trolls so you can hunt here well as a lower level. But be careful there's a Minotaur Archer and a Minotaur Mage in the lower level of the cave that could be lured, even to the surface.

It also includes a grave with this inscription:

You read: Deraut kadet ul al cadert!

Herot kar Gurxat

Also, there you can find Daily Respawns of 12 Troll Green and other items, they're located on a small room filled with bugs and bats, west of the entrance.

The Longsword Quest can be found in this cave.
