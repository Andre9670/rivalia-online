## Overview

- **City:** Venore
- **Location:** Below of Necromant House, here (coords 128.14, 126.93).
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Recommended level:** Knights 60 / Paladins 50 / Mages 50
- **Skills:** Knights 60 / Paladins 60 / Mages 50
- **Experience:** Bad ★★
- **Profit / Loot:** Bad ★★
- **Best loot:** Ancient Shield, Guardian Shield, Stone Skin Amulet, Mind Stone, Demonic Skeletal Hand
- **Creatures:** Ghost, Demon Skeleton

Although not an easily accessible hunting location, it can be an alternative spot for those seeking to complete the bestiary. 

The player must know "how to get there" to be able to hunt. 

If the player takes the correct path and avoids the giant spiders, they will find a location with a large number of demon skeletons. Mages can take advantage of this because they don't sense invisible characters and can use the invisibility spell. 

Knights, paladins, and monks should carry rings to become invisible in critical moments. It is recommended to carry weapons with elemental damage to escape being surrounded by ghosts. 

There are no food sources in the area. It is recommended to bring some with you. 

The location has a rich history and is frequently visited by players. Be careful.

***Warning:** Do not go through the portal with the magic wall. From there, you will encounter creatures that should only be faced by higher-level characters.*

### History

This unnamed underground base was established by the Nightmare Knights to guard the Nightmare Pits (Pits of Inferno), as suggested by this and this book. It was built underneath Goshnar's grave, directly south in connection to the Shrine of Wonders. The base was constructed to support a large number of fighters, having all the necessary facilities, including a convenient teleportal hub. However, the base is now abandoned and overrun by undead, and often overlooked by the many brave adventurers who seek their way into the Pits of Inferno.

- The entrance and exit, just below Goshnar's grave. Shrine of Wonders to the north.
- The main hallway. Shrine of Wonders to the north. Kitchen area, dining hall and dorm to the west. Teleportals hub to the east. Study to the south.
- The teleportals hub, now out of order, used to take you to Thais, Carlin, Kazordoon, Ab'Dendriel and Darama.
- The kitchen, with the connecting "freezer" to the north-west.
- The well and "freezer".
- The dining hall. Main hallway to the east, main dorm to the west.
- The main dorm.
- The armoury, south of the main hallway, on the way to the study.
- The study with additional beds, furthest south. Some books can be found here (see Venore Libraries page).
