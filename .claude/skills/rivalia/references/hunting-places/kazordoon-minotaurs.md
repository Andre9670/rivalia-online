## Overview

- **Implemented (Tibia version):** 6.61-6.97
- **City:** Kazordoon
- **Location:** West of Kazordoon, here.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **EXP rating:** Average
- **Loot rating:** Average
- **Notable loot:** Wand of Cosmic Energy, Minotaur Trophy, Taurus Mace, Double Axe, Bear Paw
- **Recommended level / skills:**
  - **Knights:** level 20, skills 35, def 35
  - **Paladins:** level 15, skills 35, def 35
  - **Mages:** level 15, skills 30, def 20
- **For the tower — recommended level / skills:**
  - **Knights:** level 30, skills 55, def 55
  - **Paladins:** level 30, skills 50, def 50
  - **Mages:** level 35, skills 35, def 20

The Kazordoon Minotaurs are west of the city, just outside the Dwarf Mines. The Minotaurs are led by The Horned Fox, who leads raids on the mines.

From the main entrance of Kazordoon go from the red X mark in the top right, following the white line to a rope hole, here there are some spiders. Go north and exit down a ramp where there are Poison Spiders and some Dwarves on the ground level.

Keep following the white line south across two bridges and past other mine entrances. You may encounter Snakes, Poison Spiders, Wolves, and Bears. Here use your shovel on a Loose Stone Pile to go down.

On the **ground** level inside the tower are: 2 Minotaur Guards, 2 Minotaur Archers.

Outside, around the tower are 4 Minotaurs.

Outside the south entrance are 3 Minotaur Archers, Wolves, Deer.

 **Underground -1** has many Minotaur Archers, Minotaurs, Bats.

On the map: the red X mark is the way out towards Dwarf Mines

The northern up arrow mark leads to a dead end above ground with 3 Bears and some trees.

The second up arrow mark goes to the grassy area around the Minotaur Tower.

The third up arrow mark goes into the Minotaur Tower.

The fourth up arrow mark goes to the peninsula south of the mines, where there are some Archers and a way into Undead Caves.

 **Underground -2** has many Minotaur Archers, Minotaurs, Bats.

The far south end has the teleport to The Horned Fox's room, which is just south of it.
 **second**: 4 Minotaur Guards, 4 Minotaur Archers
