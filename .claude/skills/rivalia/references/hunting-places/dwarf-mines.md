# Dwarf Mines

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.


## Overview

- **Implemented:** 6.1
- **City:** Kazordoon
- **Location:** West and below Kazordoon.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Recommended level:** Knights 8 / Paladins 13 / Mages 14
- **Recommended skills:** Knights 30 / Paladins 30 / Mages 12
- **Recommended defence:** Knights 30 / Mages 15
- **Loot:** Good (4/5)
- **Experience:** Average (3/5)

### Sub-area requirements

**For Dwarf Guards**
- Level: Knights 30 / Paladins 30 / Mages 29
- Skills: Knights 60 / Paladins 60
- Defence: Knights 60


The dwarf mines are a group of caves located near or inside the city of Kazordoon. They can be divided in mines reachable outside Kazordoon and mines which can only be accessed by going through Kazordoon. The mines outside Kazordoon have 6 entrances resulting in 6 different caves. On the third floor 4 of those tunnels are connected through the Mine Hub.

In those places, you'll mainly find all kinds of Dwarves, but there are also some less frequent creatures dwelling deep in the caves. While exploring the mines, you should be careful as you can easily access the Caves of the Lost to fight harder dwarves in the deepest floor of some mines.

It is also known that there is a tunnel that takes from Mount Sternum to the mines and the other way around.

The mines have many mysteries, including, but not limited to, the Basilisk and some quests.

The Steam Boat is located deep inside the mines under the city of Kazordoon.

You can find a mysterious site that was built by the Planegazers.

Crusader Helmet Quest, Circle Room Quest and Iron Ore Quest take place here. This place is also a part of Sam's Old Backpack Quest, Kissing a Pig Quest, Opticording Sphere Quest and To Outfox a Fox Quest.

### Through Kazordoon
There is a main part which connects different mines:

***One** marks the mine to the (unreachable) Basilisk cave, you will meet Slimes and Poison Spiders.
***Two** marks a mine with some Carrion Worms, Dwarf Soldiers and Dwarf Guards. There are also The Lost further down.
***Three** marks a mine with Carrion Worms, Dwarf Soldiers and Dwarf Guards. Nothing was added to this mine in the Update 9.80.
***Four** marks a mine with Carrion Worms, Dwarf Soldiers and Dwarf Guards. You can also find the Snow White Room in this mine. Nothing was added to this mine in the Update 9.80.
***Five** marks a new area with Wyverns, Earth Elementals, Giant Spiders and Drillworms.
