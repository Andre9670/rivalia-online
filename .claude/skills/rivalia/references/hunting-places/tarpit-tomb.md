# Tarpit Tomb

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

## Overview

- **Implemented:** 7.3
- **City:** Ankrahmun
- **Location:** Below the Kha'labal desert, here.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Experience:** Average (3/5)
- **Loot:** Good (4/5)
- **Notable loot:** Ravager's Axe, Mini Mummy, Demonbone Amulet, Boots of Haste, Steel Boots

### Recommended Levels & Skills

- **Knights:** Level 25+, skill 65, def 60
- **Paladins:** Level 20+, skill 60
- **Mages:** Level 20+

### Area-Specific Requirements

- **Second Floor:**
  - **Knights:** Level 30+, skill 60, def 60
  - **Paladins:** Level 25+, skill 60
  - **Mages:** Level 25+
- **Third Floor (Past Teleporter):**
  - **Knights:** Level 60+, skill 70, def 70
  - **Paladins:** Level 50+, skill 70
  - **Mages:** Level 50+
- **Fourth Floor (Past teleporter):**
  - **Knights:** Level 80+, skill 80, def 80
  - **Paladins:** Level 90+, skill 80
  - **Mages:** Level 70+

The name *Tarpit Tomb* comes from the Tar  (black oil-like stuff) where the entrance to this tomb is covered with. The name is made up by players, CipSoft does not have an official name for it.

If the players on the first hunting level (see map link at bottom of page) avoid the Crypt Shambler at "E", do not go further passed the point of "K" and also avoid the Bonelord marked at "Q" on the map, a Sorcerer can easily hunt this area from leaving the Isle of Destiny by running the monsters and using their Wand.
