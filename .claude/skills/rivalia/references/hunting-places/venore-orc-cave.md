# Venore Orc Cave

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

- **Implemented:** 7.1
- **City:** Venore
- **Location:** East of Venore, here.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Recommended level:** Knights 13, Paladins 13, Mages 13
- **Recommended skills:** Knights 30, Paladins 30
- **Recommended defense:** Knights 30, Paladins 30
- **Experience:** Bad (2/5)
- **Profit/Loot:** Bad (2/5)
- **Notable loot:** Wand of Decay, Orc Tooth, Shamanic Hood, Broken Shamanic Staff, Orc Leather

This cave is located in the swamp east of Venore. Filled with a small variety of weaker Orcs, it definitely is a great place to hunt for lower levels. 
To hunt in this cave, you will need a shovel and a rope.

In this cave, you will find the Orc Shaman Quest.

**Surface creatures:**

- Snake
- Orc
- Orc Spearman

- Orc
