## Overview

- **Location:** Below the Jakundaf Desert (entrance (here — 127.137/125.94/7)).
- **Nearest City:** Kazordoon
- **Recommended Level:** Knights 30, Paladins 25, Mages 25
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Recommended Skills:** Knights 60, Paladins 60
- **Profit / Exp:** Loot/Profit: Average (★★★); Exp: Bad (★★)
- **Notable Loot:** Bonelord Eye, Terra Rod, Wand of Cosmic Energy, Bonelord Shield, Necrotic Rod
- **Implemented:** 6.1

The **Desert Dungeon** caves form a maze in which even experienced players can get lost. It is located under the Jakundaf Desert. Once in the caves, there are only two possibilities to exit the Desert Dungeon: by completing the Desert Dungeon Quest or by using the teleporter near entrance to the quest room. There are many different rooms in this maze containing different kinds of creatures which are dangerous for unprepared and inexperienced players.

The Desert Dungeon Library, the Hall of Lost Doors, and the Teleport Room are all located here and part of the Desert Dungeon Quest. The dungeon also houses three NPCs, the necromancer Adrenius, the adventurer Hagor, and the blind Nemal, who also play their roles in the quest.

There are still many mysteries and possibly unsolved puzzles found here. It is where you will find the famous unreachable room with a lever and a teleport, guarded by a single Banshee ((here — 127.107/125.100/12)), as well as mysterious books, keys, levers and switch tiles.
