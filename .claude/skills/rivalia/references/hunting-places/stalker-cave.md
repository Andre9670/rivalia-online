## Overview

- **City:** Thais
- **Location:** South of Thais, here.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Recommended level:** Knights 30 / Paladins 30 / Mages 28
- **Skills:** Knights 50 / Paladins 60
- **Experience:** Bad ★★
- **Profit / Loot:** Bad ★★
- **Creatures:** Stalker, Banshee, Ghoul

The **Stalker Cave** is located south of Thais. Here's another entrace for Banshee's room in White Flower Temple.
To get it, you must pass throught Minotaur Camp and west of Cyclops Camp.
To go there, you must bring a Pick and (at least) 3 big items (e.g. Parcel, Box, etc.) to climb a hill.

### How to enter
