> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

## Overview

- **Implemented:** 5.1
- **City:** Venore
- **Location:** Below Plains of Havoc, here.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Experience:** Average (3/5)
- **Loot:** Average (3/5)
- **Notable loot:** Dragon Scale Mail, Royal Helmet, Dragon Slayer, Dragon Lord Trophy, Tower Shield

### Recommended Levels & Skills

- **Knights:** Level 80+, skill 70, def 65
- **Paladins:** Level 65+, skill 70
- **Mages:** Level 70+, skill 50, def Fire

The Plains of Havoc dragon lair is **west** from the temple.

- 1 Dragon
- 2 Dragons
- 4 Dragons and  2 Dragon Lords

Demodras occasionally spawns on the third floor. You can also find here daily spawns of a Small Diamond, Small Emerald, a Small Amethyst, and a War Hammer.

This cave is really not a great place to hunt by itself, but you will often see paladins who hunt Giant Spiders in PoH hunting in this cave as well. A level 65 pally with a decent amount of supplies could clear this cave easily, luring one thing at a time. Or a 80+ knight could also clear it easily, although with a lot more waste.
