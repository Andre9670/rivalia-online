## Overview

- **Implemented:** 6.1
- **City:** Kazordoon
- **Location:** West of Orc Land; east of Ferngrims Gate.
- **Recommended level:** Knights 55 / Paladins 50 / Mages 45
- **Skills:** Knights 70 / Paladins 70
- **Experience:** Bad
- **Profit / Loot:** Bad
- **Creatures:** Deer, Wasp, Rorc

The **Rorc Plains** is an area between Ferngrims Gate and the Orc Land, here (coords 128.34, 124.52). It's inhabited mostly by Rorcs, but some weaker creatures also roam it. 

This area of the map has existed for many years, since the addition of Kazordoon, however, it only became inhabited by Rorcs much later, with the 10.10 update.

**Creatures in Ferngrims Gate:**
- Deer
- Wasp
- Rorc
