**Ghoul Hill** is a small hill with Ghouls in Edron, west of Cyclopolis
Be aware! You have to go past Hunters encampment to get there. Northeast from the Ghoul Hill is a small graveyard, filled with Skeletons and Ghouls.

The Ghoul hill can be a good place for experience and loot for newbie mages/paladins, since the ghouls there can be ran around quite easily. Experienced players should consider hunting bigger spawns.
