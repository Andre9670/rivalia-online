# The Battlefields

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

The Battlefields is a stage in the Demon Forge and is the route to Latrivan's and Golgordan's chambers. This is the second and final stage leading to the two demon bosses. Players arrive here after navigating The Vats. After slaying the demon bosses, players gain access to The Arcanum.

**See also:** Demon Forge, The Vats, The Arcanum, Latrivan, Golgordan
