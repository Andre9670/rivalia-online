# Forsaken Mine

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

The **Forsaken Mine** is located south of the gate to Ab'Dendriel, here (coords 127.136, 123.244), and can change daily to contain different creatures.

Known cave versions are:
* **Forsaken Mine/Rorcs**
** containing Rorcs.
* **Forsaken Mine/Forest Furies**
** containing Forest Furies, Leaf Golems and Wilting Leaf Golems.
* **Forsaken Mine/Cyclopes**
** containing Cyclopes, Cyclops Drones and Cyclops Smiths.
* **Forsaken Mine/Lost Dwarves**
** containing Drillworms, Lost Hushers, Lost Throwers and Lost Bashers.
