# Peninsula Tomb

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

## Overview

- **Implemented:** 7.3
- **City:** Ankrahmun
- **Location:** Below the Kha'labal desert, on the south-west peninsula, here.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Experience:** Good (4/5)
- **Loot:** Average (3/5)
- **Notable loot:** Mini Mummy, Boots of Haste, Hammer of Wrath, Vampire Shield, Noble Axe

### Recommended Levels & Skills

- **Knights:** Level 20+, skill 45, def 40
- **Paladins:** Level 15+, skill 45
- **Mages:** Level 13+

### Area-Specific Requirements

- **Second Floor:**
  - **Knights:** Level 30+, skill 60, def 60
  - **Paladins:** Level 25+, skill 60
  - **Mages:** Level 25+
- **Third Floor (Past Teleporter):**
  - **Knights:** Level 60+, skill 70, def 70
  - **Paladins:** Level 50+, skill 70
  - **Mages:** Level 50+
- **Fourth Floor (Past teleporter):**
  - **Knights:** Level 80+, skill 80, def 80
  - **Paladins:** Level 90+, skill 80
  - **Mages:** Level 70+

The name *Peninsula Tomb* comes from the Peninsula (island connected to land) where the entrance to this tomb lies on. The name is made up by players, CipSoft does not have an official name for it. You will have to dig a few times to open the hole. The deeper you go the harder the monsters are. This tomb is probably the best tomb to start, since it's close to the city and has good spawns.

The deeper levels are great and relatively risk free places to farm Vampire Dust at Mid-levels, just remember to bring a lot of area runes to deal with the Stalkers.
