## Overview

- **Implemented:** 7.3
- **City:** Ankrahmun
- **Location:** Below the Kha'labal desert, near the GS hill, (x:129.108, y:127.56, z:7).
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Recommended level:** Knights 10 / Paladins 10 / Mages 13
- **Recommended skills:** Knights 45 / Paladins 45
- **Recommended defence:** Knights 40
- **Loot:** Unknown
- **Experience:** Unknown
- **Notable loot:** Pharaoh Sword, Maxilla Maximus, Mini Mummy, Boots of Haste, Vampire Shield

*See also: Ankrahmun Tombs*

The name *Mountain Tomb* comes from the Mountains  where the entrance to this tomb is very close to. The name is made up by players, CipSoft does not have an official name for it. The deeper you go, the harder the monsters are. It is also known as the "GS Tomb", due to the Giant Spider that can be seen running on a hill.
