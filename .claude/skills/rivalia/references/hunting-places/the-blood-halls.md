# The Blood Halls

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

The Blood Halls is the a stage of the Demon Forge and is the route to The Foundry. This is the first stage leading to the demon boss Madareth. Players arrive here after navigating The Sunken Cave and slaying Zugurosh.

**See also:** Demon Forge, The Sunken Cave, The Foundry, Madareth
