## Overview

- **Implemented (Tibia version):** 6.4
- **City:** Edron
- **Location:** West of the Edron castle, here.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **EXP rating:** Average
- **Loot rating:** Average
- **Notable loot:** Wolf Paw, Silver Amulet, Small Stone
- **Recommended level / skills:**
  - **Knights:** level 8, skills 25, def 30
  - **Paladins:** level 8, skills 20
  - **Mages:** level 7

This area is west of the Edron castle.  It can be reached through an underground passage accessible in the cellar of the south-west tower of the Edron Castle.

This is a good hunting area for characters starting their adventuring career in Edron.

On the north side of the peninsula is a Troll cave which is home to the Edron Troll Quest.

On the south side of the peninsula is a Goblin cave with the Edron Goblin Quest.  There is also a large White Mushroom daily spawn inside the cave.

On the west end, there is a single tunnel called the Lost Mines which is part of the Sea of Light Quest.
