## Overview

- **Implemented (Tibia version):** 5.2
- **City:** Rookgaard
- **Location:** East of Rookgaard (here).
- **Vocation:** No Vocation.
- **Notable loot:** Bear Paw, Honeycomb
- **Recommended level / skills:**
  - **Knights:** level 5
  - **Paladins:** level 5
  - **Mages:** level 5

The **Bear Cave** is the common name for the cave east of the town of Rookgaard. It contains a total of 7 Bears and even houses a secret quest. This is the best place on the island of Rookgaard for gathering Bear Paws.
Beware though, it is dangerous to go in without the appropriate level and equipment.
Home of the Dragon Corpse Quest.
