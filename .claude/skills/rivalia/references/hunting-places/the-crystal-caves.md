# The Crystal Caves

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

The Crystal Caves is a stage in the Demon Forge and is the route to The Mirror Maze of Madness. It is the first stage leading to the demon boss Zugurosh. Players arrive here after slaying Ushuriel in The Dark Path.

**See also:** Demon Forge, The Dark Path, The Mirror Maze of Madness, Zugurosh
