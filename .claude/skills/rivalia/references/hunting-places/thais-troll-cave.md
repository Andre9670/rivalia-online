# Thais Troll Cave

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

- **Implemented:** 5.2
- **City:** Thais
- **Location:** East of Thais, here.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Recommended level:** Knights 8, Paladins 8, Mages 8
- **Recommended skills:** Knights 15, Paladins 15, Mages 15
- **Recommended defense:** Knights 15, Paladins 15, Mages 15
- **Experience:** Very Bad (1/5)
- **Profit/Loot:** Very Bad (1/5)
- **Notable loot:** Honeycomb, Orc Tooth, Wolf Paw, Skull Belt, Silver Amulet

The entrance is a hole on the north side of a small mountain. Once you go down the hole you can choose to go right or left. Left side leads to Trolls, right leads to Orcs. The two side merge on the third level.

The Dead Archer Quest can be completed here.

There is a back entrance in the middle of the mountain, but it doesn't lead to the main cave, it leads to the Silver Amulet Quest instead.
