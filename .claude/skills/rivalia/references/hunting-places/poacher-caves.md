# Poacher Caves

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

The **Poacher Caves** are located in Ferngrims Gate, north of the Green Claw Swamp (here (coords 127.243, 124.184)), and can change daily to contain different creatures as they're part of a Poacher Caves Mini World Change.

There are three variations on these caves:
* **Poacher Caves/Game**
** containing Wolves, War Wolves, Bears, and Boars;
* **Poacher Caves/Poachers**
** containing Poachers and Hunters;
* **Poacher Caves/Gloomy**
** containing Ghost Wolves and Gloom Wolves.

Additionaly, regardless of the varation the northern cave on level -4 is always populated by Wyverns.

Poacher Caves is a Varying Monster Spawn.
