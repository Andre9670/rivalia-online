The **Ashta'daramai** or *Blue Djinn Fortress* is located in the Kha'zeel (Ankrahmun Desert). This is where Gabel reinforces his troops to repel Malor and his Green Djinn and Efreets, hoping to eventually defeat them entirely and overcome the Mal'ouquah. The blue djinn are friendly towards humans, but remain reclusive and have limited dealings with them. Because of the war between the Marids and Efreets, they will not trade with anyone they do not trust entirely.

Once you have gained their trust by completing the Blue Djinn Quest, you can buy and sell from Nah'Bob (weapons) and Haroun (magical items).

Also housed in the Ashta'daramai with the Blue Djinn and Marids are Stone Golems, Scarabs, and Fire Elementals.

To reach the Ashta'daramai, simply follow the ramp into the mountains north of Ankrahmun ((x:129.96, y:127.66, z:7)). Be careful of Stone Golems that wander on that path.

**Note:** *only players with **level 30+** may pass the entrance doors.*
