# Orc Peninsula

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

## Overview

- **City:** Thais
- **Location:** North of Thais and east of Greenshore, here.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Experience:** Average (3/5)
- **Loot:** Average (3/5)
- **Notable loot:** Wolf Trophy, Light Bandana, Orc Tooth, Obsidian Lance, Halberd

### Recommended Levels & Skills

- **Knights:** Level 30+, skill 60, def 60
- **Paladins:** Level 25+, skill 60
- **Mages:** Level 27+

The small peninsula east of Greenshore and north of Thais containing an Orc Berserker, 3 Orc Riders, 3 Orc Warriors, 3 Orc Spearmen and 6 War Wolves. This place is perfect for mages because at level 27 or more they can summon a monk, the best monster to use there because of its constant healing. Also there are no monsters that can see invisible. Before the Spring patch 2009 it was possible to hunt here at level 20 by luring the orc berserker and orc riders and stairhopping them.

**Creatures:**
- Orc
- Orc Spearman
- Orc Warrior
- War Wolf
- Orc Rider
- Orc Berserker

Newbies should be careful here, people often tend to lure the Orc Berserker and the Orc Riders to the staircase, and then block the newbies so the lurers can kill them and loot their bodies. With the large amount of bodies and loot lying around, the lurers may also put them under themselves to prevent being pushed by the newbies.
