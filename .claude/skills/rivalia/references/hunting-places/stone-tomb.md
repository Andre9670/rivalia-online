> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

## Overview

- **Implemented:** 7.3
- **City:** Ankrahmun
- **Location:** Below the Kha'labal desert, here.
- **Vocation:** All vocations.
- **Experience:** Unknown
- **Loot:** Unknown
- **Notable loot:** Djinn Blade, Mini Mummy, Boots of Haste, Vampire Shield, Noble Axe

### Recommended Levels & Skills

- **Knights:** Level 15+, skill 55, def 50
- **Paladins:** Level 15+, skill 55
- **Mages:** Level 13+

### Area-Specific Requirements

- **Second Floor:**
  - **Knights:** Level 30+, skill 60, def 60
  - **Paladins:** Level 25+, skill 60
  - **Mages:** Level 25+
- **Third Floor (Past Teleporter):**
  - **Knights:** Level 60+, skill 70, def 70
  - **Paladins:** Level 50+, skill 70
  - **Mages:** Level 50+
- **Fourth Floor (Past teleporter):**
  - **Knights:** Level 80+, skill 80, def 80
  - **Paladins:** Level 90+, skill 80
  - **Mages:** Level 70+

The name *Stone Tomb* comes from the Stones  where the entrance to this tomb is covered with. The name is made up by players, CipSoft calls it Thalas' Venomous Pit. The third floor of the tomb is the best third floor for Knights as it has no Ghosts or Stalkers. On the third floor one can make 80k/hour experience and get good loot. Don't bring heavy food as there's an abundance of Scarabs to supply you with meat.
