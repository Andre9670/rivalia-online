# The Dark Path

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

The Dark Path is a long strip of molten rock with many Pyro-Elementals. It is the first area of the Demon Forge, accessed by going up a staircase at The Retreat. On the quest, you must go through The Dark Path to gain entry to the other sections.

At the end of The Dark Path are stairs to the Hall of the Colossi. After the Hall of the Colossi is The Ruthless Seven Minion Ushuriel.

The Dark Path is often hunted by Druids (and to a lesser extent Sorcerers) because Hellfire Fighters are great for experience and loot. However, the spawn will not be enough for constant hunting, so mages typically kill the Hellfire Fighters below The Retreat and do a round of The Crystal Caves.
