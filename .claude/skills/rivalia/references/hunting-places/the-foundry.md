# The Foundry

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

**The Foundry** is a stage of the Demon Forge and is the route to the demon boss Madareth. This is the second and final stage leading to the demon boss. Players arrive here after navigating The Blood Halls and will gain access to The Vats when Madareth has been slain.

**See also:** Demon Forge, The Blood Halls, The Vats, Madareth
