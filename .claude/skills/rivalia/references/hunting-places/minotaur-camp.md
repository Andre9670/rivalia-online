# Minotaur Camp

> ⚠️ Source: TibiaWiki (fandom) — **Tibia 7.4-era VANILLA** (implemented ≤7.4). Rivalia is custom: creature stats/spawns/loot DIFFER — cross-check against local hunt-spots.json + catalog-stats.json and wiki.rivaliaonline.com. Use for layout/route flavor, not exact numbers.

## Overview

- **City:** Thais
- **Location:** South of Thais, next to the Cyclops Camp.
- **Vocation:** All vocations
- **Experience:** Average (3/5)
- **Loot:** Good (4/5)
- **Notable loot:** Minotaur Leather, Bronze Amulet, Minotaur Horn, Bat Wing, Antlers

### Recommended Levels & Skills

- **Knights:** Level 8+
- **Paladins:** Level 8+
- **Mages:** Level 8+

This place is located to the south of Thais, next to the Cyclops Camp.
This is a nice place for low levels to hunt. The nearest protection zone is in the water on the beach to the west

Because it is known as a good low level hunt area, some basic precaution is advised on Optional PvP worlds. Similarly, on Open PvP worlds be prepared to run to the protection zone mentioned above.
